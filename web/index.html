📘 System Architecture & Methodology

Smart India Hackathon 2026 · Problem Statement 26072 (Ministry of Earth Sciences / IMD)

⚡ Problem Statement Context (MoES/IMD PS 26072)

Lightning strikes account for over 2,500 annual fatalities in India, causing greater loss of life than floods, cyclones, and heatwaves combined. The vast majority of victims are rural farmers, agricultural workers, and outdoor laborers in Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha, Jharkhand, and West Bengal.

Current operational synoptic warnings are broad, issued at the district level with multi-hour lead times, leading to alert fatigue and low compliance. Project Vajra delivers hyper-local, block-level (12 km resolution) probabilistic nowcasts with 30–60 minutes lead time, calibrated to minimize false alarms while guaranteeing actionable response windows.

Key Engineering Guarantees:

  • Zero API Hallucination: Real data streams are distinctly tagged (LIVE / REPLAY / SIMULATION).
  • Calibrated Monotonicity: PAVA isotonic calibration guarantees that higher forecast probability always corresponds to higher observed lightning frequency.
  • Dual-Track AI: Rapid optical/Kalman tracking for immediate 0–30m trajectory combined with non-linear gradient-boosted decision trees for 30–60m convective intensification.
  • SACHET / CAP 1.2: Native XML and JSON broadcast packages compliant with ITU-T X.1303 for automated mobile siren and SMS gateway dispatches.

🪜 4-Stage Graceful Degradation Ladder

Thunderstorm nowcasting must never crash when radar beams are blocked by terrain or satellite downlinks experience momentary packet loss. Vajra implements an automated 4-stage fallback ladder:

RUNG 1 (PRIMARY)
Full Multi-Modal Fusion
Doppler Radar (VIL/dBZ) + INSAT-3D Thermal IR + GLM/LIS Spaceborne Lightning + GFS Thermodynamics.
Confidence: 85%–95% · Resolution: High
RUNG 2
Satellite-Primary Ladder
Active in radar-sparse orographic zones (Himalayan / Northeast). Operates on INSAT-3D/3DR TIR + GLM flashes + GFS NWP.
Confidence: 65%–75% · Graceful degradation
RUNG 3
NWP & Thermodynamic Proxy
Triggered if satellite downlink drops. Computes lightning potential from GFS/NCUM CAPE, CIN, K-Index, and Lifted Index.
Confidence: 45%–55% · Macro-scale bounds
RUNG 4
Historical Climatology Baseline
30-year monthly diurnal lightning flash density baseline. Fails safe without blanking operational screens.
Confidence: 25%–35% · Fail-safe baseline
